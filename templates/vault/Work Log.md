# Work Log

Permanent session history. **Append-only, newest first.**

This is where session narratives go when they age out of the ~2-week window in the handoff. Nothing is ever deleted from here — if a claim turns out to be wrong, correct it in place and say so, don't remove the entry.

> **What belongs here:** what happened in a session, in enough detail to reconstruct why later.
> **What does not:** durable operational facts (they live in an operations note), decisions (they live in the Decisions Log — link to them), and credentials (they live only in [[Credentials and secrets]]).
